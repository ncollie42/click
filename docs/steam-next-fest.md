
  # Steam Next Fest — October 2026                                            
                                                                              
  Target: **Oct 19–26, 2026** (10:00 PT → 10:00 PT). One shot per game, ever. 
                                                                              
  Registration closes **Mon Aug 31, 11:59pm PT**. No late reg, no waitlist, no
  exceptions.                                                                 
  Today is Sat Aug 22 — 9 days.                                               
                                                                              
  Owner column: **N** = nico only (identity/payment/art/browser capture),     
  **A** = agent can do it.                                                    
                                                                              
  ## Hard dates                                                               
                                                                              
   Date       | What                                                          
  ------------|-------------------------------------------------------------  
   Mon Aug 31 | Registration deadline. Store page must already be public.     
   Mon Sep 21 | Demo build + store page submitted for review, if you want     
              | to be live for Press Preview                                  
   Mon Oct 5  | Hard deadline — everything submitted for review               
   Thu Oct 8  | Press Preview begins                                          
   Mon Oct 19 | Fest opens                                                    
   Mon Oct 26 | Fest closes. Release date must be after this.                 
                                                                              
  ## Eligibility (all must hold)                                              
                                                                              
  • Steamworks account in good standing                                       
  • Published, **public** Coming Soon store page — live *before* registering  
  • Not a prologue/chapter/demo of existing content                           
  • Playable demo live by Oct 19 (not required at registration)               
  • Release date set after Oct 26, 2026                                       
  • Register from the **base game** app, not the demo app

  Phase 1 gates Phase 2 gates Phase 3. Phase 4 runs in parallel starting now —
  it is the longest                                                           
  pole and has no dependency on Steam approval until upload time.
