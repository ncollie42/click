 The main things I will be going over for each tower are as follows:

    The Tower's stats, being click storage, range, damage and shots per second.
    Any special abilities tied to the tower.
    Pros and Cons for using the tower.

I will not be going over the Main Castle or the Camouflager (nice spelling) as the former is the objective and the latter falls under utility. However, I will be going through the defenses as there isn't many and they can be attributed to towers through their main use being dealing with enemies.

They will be in order from the point in the game that you unlock them, however it is entirely likely you will get them in some other order (looking at you, contrarians). I order them like this as its the order someone is likely to pick these towers up based on their surrounding purchases.

Without further ado, let's look at the towers.
Turret


Starting with the first tower on the list, the Turret. Here are the Stats:

Price: 5 Wood, 5 Stone
Health: 10
Damage: 5
Shots per Second: 1
Range: 1
Placement Area: 1x1
Click Capacity: 3

For a starting tower, it's nothing special. The damage, range and health are extremely low making it hard to use as an actual defense, however it comes with the upside of being extremely compact. Paired with its low price, it's might be reasonable to use in the early stages of the game.

Pros

    Cheap, Expendable and Compact.
    Can be used to chip away at portal health.

Cons

    Fragile and Weak, making it hard to use defensively.

Final Verdict: Niche, Early Game tower.

Barracks


Someone asked, "What if we send out some people to take out the enemies instead of just shooting them?" and then the Barracks was made. Here are the Stats:

Price: 20 Stone, 5 Food
Health: N/A
Damage: 20
Shots per Second: 1

"Well, they can't all be good.

The Barracks is an extremely niche tower that acts more like a utility building than a defense. It can't be targeted by enemies and sends out a unit to attack one enemy at a time. Its damage is less than that of an Outpost while supplying little defense for the enemies to push past. On top of all that, it cannot attack portals. Please buff this Sokpop."

Thank you Aran, my bad for addressing the wrong person.

In my last verdict, I said the Barracks was Disappointing. Boy was I wrong. You see, even though the damage is underwhelming despite being doubled, I way undermined its defensive capabilities. You see, being untargetable doesn't sound like much on paper, but as user ABunchOfVirgins pointed out to me in a discussion post, it is very much more than what it seems. That is all because it's not untargetable. It's Indestructible. Enemies physically cannot pass it and have to route their pathing around it. Now this allows you to do things like in the following image...


There is a wall of Barracks on every other route the enemies could take.

This strategy changes the way the game is played as now it's a matter of forcing the enemies to go into a specific area instead of assaulting you from all directions. Now there may still be flying enemies and witches that can get in the way of this, but for literally every other enemy, they are forced into a chokepoint.



Now you may be asking, "But can't all buildings not targeted by enemies reroute the pathing?"

Nope, they pass through both basic and utility buildings, and Turrets and Aggro towers are able to be targeted. This is exclusive to Barracks and will likely make it alone the most desirable tower to use until Aran decideds it's ready to be subjected to my beratement again.

Once more, special thanks to ABunchOfVirgins for pointing out my fallacies. I was very wrong.

Special: Cannot be targeted or passed by enemies. Sends out a unit to attack enemies.

Pros

    Untargetable.
    Allows for pathing manipulation.

Cons

    Slightly Expensive.
    Very low damage output.

Final Verdict: Currently the most powerful Tower.
